Why we exist

Vision and mission

Vision

A community of believers who have been delivered, developed and deployed: living in God's presence, secure in their divine identity and boldly advancing His kingdom.

Mission

To lead God's people into intimacy with Him through His Word, prayer, deliverance and holiness, equipping them to walk in divine purpose and possess their spiritual inheritance.

Our model

Our model: the Acts 2 community

Acts 2:42

“And they continued steadfastly in the apostles' doctrine and fellowship, in the breaking of bread, and in prayers.”

At Pentecost God poured out His Spirit on sons and daughters alike. The believers who followed lived as a devoted, praying, generous community. That is the community we want to be.

The Spirit poured out 2:17–18A prophetic community where sons and daughters hear God
Devoted to teaching 2:42School of Intimacy, Bible discussions
Devoted to prayer 2:42Prayer meetings, Crossover Prayers
Fellowship 2:42, 44Online gatherings, physical retreats
Breaking bread 2:46Shared meals at our physical gatherings and communion
Caring for one another 2:44–45Caring for one another in times of need
Worship and gladness 2:46–47Worship at the heart of every gathering
